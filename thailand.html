01

Arrive Bangkok. Meet-and-greet, hotel check-in, evening Chao Phraya riverside at leisure.

02

Temples & city. Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Wat Arun and canal longtail ride.

03

Safari World, then south. Full-day Safari World & Marine Park; evening flight to Krabi.

04

Krabi 4 islands. Speedboat across Chicken, Poda, Tup and Phra Nang with beach picnic.

05

Phi Phi day. Maya Bay, Viking Cave, Pileh Lagoon and Monkey Beach by cruise and longtail.

06

To Koh Samui. Fly or ferry east; Fisherman's Village evening market.

07

Angthong Marine Park. Full-day snorkeling, kayaking and Emerald Lagoon climb.

08

Departure. Breakfast, spa-hour option, assisted airport transfer home.
