Day by day

Nine days of scripture, living.

01

Arrive Tel Aviv → Galilee. Meet-and-greet, drive to Tiberias on the Sea of Galilee, evening prayer by the water.

02

Around the Lake. Capernaum, Tabgha, Mount of Beatitudes and a worship boat ride on the Sea of Galilee.

03

Baptism & the Dead Sea. Renewal service at River Jordan (Yardenit), float in the Dead Sea, ascend to Jerusalem.

04

Bethlehem. Church of the Nativity, Manger Square and the Shepherds' Field.

05

Jerusalem — the Passion. Mount of Olives, Gethsemane, Via Dolorosa and the Church of the Holy Sepulchre.

06

Jerusalem — the City. Western Wall, Jewish Quarter, Ein Karem and farewell fellowship dinner.

07

Sinai. Cross into Egypt; St. Catherine's Monastery and an optional Mount Sinai sunrise climb.

08

Nebo & Petra. Mount Nebo's panorama, Madaba mosaics, then the Siq walk to Petra's Treasury.

09

Departure. Transfer to Amman airport with group blessings and assistance home.
